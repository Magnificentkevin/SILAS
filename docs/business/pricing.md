# SILAS Pricing (draft placeholder)

**Status:** placeholder for internal planning and early conversations only —
not a finalized go-to-market price. No pricing model existed anywhere in
this repo before this note (flagged by the 2026-09-11 full-team review); the
number below exists so there's something concrete to bring to a
conversation, not a locked-in decision.

## Placeholder price

**$200/month per account (flat), introductory/pilot rate.**

"Per account" matches SILAS's own data model — `Account` is already the
unit everything else (runs, memberships, bids, integrations) is scoped to —
rather than per-seat, which doesn't fit a facility that has occasional
vendor/customer logins alongside a small number of actual staff.

## Why $200/month is a reasonable placeholder

Checked against current market pricing for adjacent categories (2026):

- **Small-business CMMS/facility-management SaaS** (the closest existing
  category to what SILAS does for the cleaning vertical today — scan/sync
  tracking, scheduling, audit trail): typically **$20–$99 per user/month**,
  with flat small-company plans around **$29–$49/month**. Source:
  [CMMS Software Cost & Pricing Guide for 2026 — Accruent](https://www.accruent.com/resources/knowledge-hub/cmms-pricing),
  [How Much Does CMMS Software Cost in 2026? — FreeMaint](https://freemaint.com/blog/how-much-does-cmms-cost)
- **SMB-friendly field service platforms** (Jobber): **$29–$249/month**
  flat, positioned explicitly as the lower-cost alternative to enterprise
  tools. Source:
  [Jobber vs. ServiceTitan — FieldPulse](https://www.fieldpulse.com/resources/blog/jobber-vs-servicetitan)
- **Enterprise field service platforms** (ServiceTitan): **$245–$500 per
  technician/month**, plus $5,000–$50,000+ implementation — not a
  comparable tier for an unproven, single-customer product. Source:
  [ServiceTitan Pricing 2026 — CostBench](https://costbench.com/software/field-service-management/servicetitan/)

$200/month per account sits above the bare-bones CMMS floor (reasonable,
since SILAS bundles scan/sync, five-point lock scheduling, BOM/vendor
bidding, and an audit-chain ledger that most entry-level CMMS plans don't
include together) and well below both Jobber's upper tier and any
enterprise-grade platform — appropriate given SILAS has zero arm's-length
customers, no case studies, and no established track record yet.
