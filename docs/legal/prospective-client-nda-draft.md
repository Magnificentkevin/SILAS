# Prospective-Client Confidentiality Agreement — DRAFT

**Status: attorney-review draft, not final, not legal advice.** This is a
one-way (unilateral) NDA — you disclosing confidential information to the
firm, not the reverse — which fits the intake-call use case. Two things to
know before sending it:

1. **The firm may decline to sign it.** Many law firms rely on their
   existing professional-responsibility obligations to prospective clients
   (see the note in the outreach letter) rather than signing a
   client-drafted NDA, or they'll offer their own intake/engagement form
   instead. That's normal, not a red flag.
2. **This does not create an attorney-client relationship** and doesn't
   obligate either side to move forward — it only protects what you share
   during the evaluation conversation.

This Agreement is entered into to permit the Disclosing Party to share
technical and business information about a software/systems invention with
the Receiving Party solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential
attorney-client engagement for patent counsel services ("**the Purpose**").

### 1. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any technical, business, or other
information disclosed by the Disclosing Party in connection with the
Purpose, including but not limited to: invention descriptions, mechanism
designs, source code, prototype details, prior-art search results,
inventorship and ownership facts, and any documents provided (including the
patent documentation packet referenced in this engagement).

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

(a) was already known to the Receiving Party without an obligation of
    confidentiality before disclosure;
(b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving
    Party;
(c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the
    Confidential Information; or
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without breach of any
    confidentiality obligation.

### 2. Obligations

The Receiving Party agrees to:

(a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(b) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party, except
    to its own attorneys, partners, or staff who need to know it for the
    Purpose and who are themselves bound by confidentiality obligations;
    and
(c) protect the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of
    care it uses for its own confidential information, and no less than
    reasonable care.

### 3. No License; No Obligation to Engage

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license, right, or
interest in the Confidential Information or any related intellectual
property. Nothing in this Agreement obligates either party to enter into
any further business relationship, engagement, or agreement.

### 4. Term

The obligations in Section 2 apply for [3 / 5] years from the Effective
Date, or until the relevant information becomes public through proper
means (for example, patent publication), whichever comes first.

### 5. Return or destruction of materials

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party will
return or destroy all materials containing Confidential Information,
except copies retained solely for legal/compliance record-keeping
purposes and subject to this Agreement's ongoing confidentiality terms.

### 6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida, without
regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

### 7. Entire agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties regarding its
subject matter and supersedes any prior discussions on confidentiality for
the Purpose. It may only be modified in writing signed by both parties.
