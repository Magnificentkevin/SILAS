## 2. Information we collect

### Account information
Email address, name, and a securely hashed password (we never store your
password in plain text). If you're a customer, vendor, or host client, your
account is also linked to the specific facility/account you belong to — we
do not give you visibility into other customers' accounts.

### Location information
If you use the field mobile application, we collect GPS coordinates at the
time you scan an asset, so that scan records can be matched to a facility
geofence. This location data is tied to the scan event, not continuously
tracked in the background.

### Voice recordings
The field mobile application lets you optionally attach a voice note to a
scan. If you record one, that audio file is uploaded and stored on our
servers, linked to that scan record.

### Business and transaction data
Depending on your role, we process facility/account records, service
pipeline and scheduling data, pricing estimates, and financial settlement
records (invoicing, ledger entries, and payment authorization status). We
use a sandboxed payment provider during the platform's current pilot phase.

### Third-party integration data
If your account connects SILAS to a third-party system (for example, a
facility-management or scheduling platform), we store an encrypted
credential for that connection and exchange data with that third party on
your behalf, limited to what's needed to perform the integration (for
example, creating a work order).

### Health-related business context
If your facility is designated as a healthcare site, we record your
acceptance of a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) where applicable. **A
recorded BAA acceptance does not by itself mean every part of the platform
has completed a HIPAA compliance assessment for your specific use** — if
you intend to transmit Protected Health Information through SILAS, please
contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] before doing so, so we can confirm what's
appropriate for your workflow.

### Technical and usage data
Standard technical logs (timestamps, error records) generated by normal
operation of the API and applications.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide and operate the platform's core functions: orchestrating
  workflows across your connected systems, scheduling, pricing, and
  settlement.
- To authenticate you and enforce that your access is limited to your own
  account/role (see our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) for how
  roles work).
- To maintain the platform's audit trail — a tamper-evident record of
  transactions and state changes, which exists to protect all parties in a
  dispute, including you.
- To communicate with you about your account or the service.
- To improve and secure the platform.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. How we share information

- **With the third-party systems you connect** — only the data necessary
  to perform the integration you've configured.
- **With service providers** who help us operate the platform (for example,
  cloud hosting and database providers), under obligations to protect your
  data.
- **Within your own account/organization** — staff with the appropriate
  role for your account can see data associated with it; we do not share
  your account's data with a different customer, vendor, or host client.
- **If required by law**, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of
  the Company, our users, or the public.
- **In connection with a business transfer** (merger, acquisition, or sale
  of assets), subject to this policy or a successor policy.
